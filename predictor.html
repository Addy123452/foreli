How it works

A deliberately simple, explainable scoring model.

1

Team strength

Each team has an estimated strength value (home side gets a small home-advantage boost).

2

Three-way scoring

Home, draw, and away scores are compared with a softmax, producing probabilities that sum to 1.

3

Explain it

Every card shows the key factors behind the estimate — so you can judge it, not just trust it.
